Privacy Policy for SlovoBox

Effective Date: January 2026

SlovoBox is owned and published by Jack Luck Games, LLC. We believe your learning journey should be private. This policy outlines our commitment to your data security.

1. Data Collection & Usage

SlovoBox does not collect, track, or transmit any personal data. We do not require account creation, and we do not have access to your vocabulary progress, confidence ratings, or language settings.

2. On-Device Storage

All of your data (word ratings, progress, and settings) is stored locally on your device using encrypted system storage. If you delete the app, this data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered by us.

3. In-App Purchases

Purchases (such as "Support the Developer") are handled exclusively by the Apple App Store. Jack Luck Games, LLC does not store your credit card or billing information. These transactions are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Analytics

We do not use third-party tracking SDKs, advertising services, or analytics tools. Your usage of the app is completely anonymous to us.

5. Contact Us

For questions regarding this policy, please contact us at jackluckgamesllc@gmail.com.
