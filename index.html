Our apps (SlovoBox and SlovoPop) do not collect, track, or transmit personal data. We use zero third-party analytics and zero advertising SDKs. Your learning journey is yours alone.

Local-Only Progress

All progress—including mastered words, levels, and high scores—is stored directly on your device using UserDefaults. Deleting the app deletes this data. We have no way to see or restore it because we never touch it.
